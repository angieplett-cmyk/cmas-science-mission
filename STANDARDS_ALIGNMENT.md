## Notes

- Six official items (S1Q10–S1Q13, S2Q1, S3Q17) have no PE code in the extracted metadata; the scoring guide lists Evidence Outcome SC.5.2.1.a whose text ends "(5-LS1-1)", so they are tagged 5-LS1-1.
- Where the scoring guide says "SEP/CCC not assessed in this item", the item gets no SEP/CCC tag (it still counts toward its PE).
- NWEA sample items use the alignment NWEA prints on its public fact sheet. Four of those are not Grade 5 PEs (N1 4-LS1-1, N3 4-ESS1-1, N5 K-PS2-1, N6 4-PS3-2); they show on the dashboard but not on the student "My Standards" Grade 5 list. N3: the sheet prints "4-LS1-1" but quotes the 4-ESS1-1 statement, so it is tagged 4-ESS1-1. N2 and N9 are described by NWEA as "a progression from" 5-LS2-1 / 5-ESS3-1.
- M5 (original) is tagged 5-PS1-1 (dissolved sugar = particles too small to see); it could also be read as 5-PS1-2.
- Strategy Missions (T1–T20) teach test-taking skills and carry no content standard.
